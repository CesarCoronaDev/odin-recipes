Chicken Cordon Bleu Casserole for One

Description

Enjoy all the comforting flavors of classic Chicken Cordon Bleu in this delicious casserole made just for one! Tender, juicy chicken is topped with smoky diced ham, melted Gruyère cheese, and a creamy Dijon mustard sauce, then finished with a golden, buttery panko breadcrumb topping sprinkled with Parmesan. A touch of fresh thyme and cayenne pepper adds the perfect hint of seasoning, making every bite rich, savory, and satisfying. Perfect for a cozy night in, this single-serving recipe delivers a restaurant-worthy meal without the hassle of preparing a large casserole!

Ingredients

Crumb Topping

  • 1/3 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 tablespoon finely grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 pinch freshly ground black pepper

Chicken

  • 1 large skinless, boneless chicken breast (about 8 ounces)
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper
  • 1 pinch fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 tablespoons heavy cream
  • 1 rounded teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 ounce diced smoked ham
  • 1 ounce Gruyere cheese, grated

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
  2. For topping, place panko in a small bowl; add in butter, Parmesan, and season with pepper. Stir together until evenly combined and mixture resembles coarse sand; set aside.
  3. Spoon crumb mixture evenly over the top. Poke crumbs down lightly into the chicken mixture with the tip of the spoon before evening out the top.
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until chicken is cooked through, the casserole is bubbling around the edges, and the top is nicely browned, about 25 minutes. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.
