Cookies and Cream Dump Cake

Description

Indulge in this irresistible Cookies and Cream Dump Cake, a decadent chocolate dessert loaded with crushed cream-filled sandwich cookies and rich, fudgy cake. Sweetened condensed milk and fluffy whipped topping create a creamy, dreamy layer, while slices of buttery goodness and semisweet chocolate chips make every bite extra indulgent. Easy to prepare with just a handful of ingredients, this crowd-pleasing dessert is perfect for parties, family gatherings, or whenever you're craving something sweet. Serve it warm with an extra dollop of whipped topping for the ultimate cookies-and-cream treat!

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 32 cream-filled chocolate sandwich cookies (such as Oreos), divided
  • 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 (8-ounce) container frozen whipped topping, thawed, plus more for serving
  • 1 (15.25-ounce) package devil’s food cake mix
  • 3/4 cup salted butter, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (180 degrees C). Coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
  2. Arrange 24 of the cookies in a single layer in the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Drizzle sweetened condensed milk evenly over the cookies, using a spatula to spread milk into gaps between the cookies.
  3. Spoon whipped topping over sweetened condensed milk, and spread into an even layer.
  4. Sprinkle cake mix evenly over whipped topping. Arrange butter pieces evenly over cake mix. Coarsely crush the remaining 8 cookies by hand, and sprinkle evenly over the top. Sprinkle chocolate chips evenly on top.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until top is set and filling is bubbling around the edges, 35 to 40 minutes. Let cool slightly, about 5 minutes, then serve hot with additional whipped topping if desired.
